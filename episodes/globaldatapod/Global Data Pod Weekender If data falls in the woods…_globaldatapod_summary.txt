The Fed's dual mandate of full employment and price stability will continue to guide policy decisions. However, inflation concerns are growing, particularly given the recent uptick in core inflation and the potential for higher prices to erode purchasing power.

The current macro environment is characterized by a divided committee at the Fed, where neither side has enough conviction to sway the chair's decision. The next step for monetary policy will depend on inflation data, with two potential easing scenarios: a pause or a slowing of rate hikes.
